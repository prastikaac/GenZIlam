Election Area No In Ilam

Election Area No 1 : In Election Area No 1, it includes all wards of Sandakpur Rural Municipality, Maijogmai Rural Municipality, and Rong Rural Municipality, as well as all wards of Suryodaya Municipality. Additionally, it covers Wards 10, 13, and 14 of Ilam Municipality and Wards 1–5, 9, and 10 of Mai Municipality.

Election Area No 2 : In Election Area No 2, it includes all wards of Chulachuli Rural Municipality, Mangsebung Rural Municipality, and Phakphokthum Rural Municipality, as well as all wards of Deumai Municipality. Additionally, it covers Wards 1–9, 11, and 12 of Ilam Municipality and Wards 6–8 of Mai Municipality.
